Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Isn't this just another agency running ads?

No. Agencies run traffic. We build the full machine that makes traffic convert — offer, authority, funnel, follow-up and sales support — and we run the paid authority layer that lifts your whole ecosystem. Ads are the fuel, not the machine.

I already have funnels, setters and tools. How is this different?

That's exactly who we do our best work for. Most established businesses don't need more pieces — they need the pieces connected and the noise removed. We simplify what you've bolted together and add the authority layer that makes it all work better.

Why the $1 to book a call?

It's a seriousness filter, not a revenue play. It keeps spam and no-shows off the calendar, protects the quality of every call, and helps our ads optimise toward people who actually take action. It's refundable.

Do I need an ad budget?

Yes. The machine runs on paid attention — most clients run around $3k/month through their own ad account. That's your fuel, spent gradually, not paid to us. If funding traffic isn't realistic yet, we're likely not the right fit right now, and we'll tell you honestly.

Is this done-for-you or coaching?

Done-for-you and implemented-for-you. We build and run it; you stay involved in decisions and take the sales calls. You work directly with our team — no group calls, no course to grind through.

What does the call involve?

It's a Client Acquisition Diagnosis, not a pitch-fest. We audit your offer and acquisition path, map the gap to consistent months, and show you what we'd build. If it's a fit both ways, we'll discuss how we work together. Either way you leave with clarity.
